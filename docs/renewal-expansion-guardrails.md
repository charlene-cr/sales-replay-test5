# Renewal Expansion Guardrails

This guardrail ledger helps teams distinguish expansion-ready renewals from accounts
that still need value proof or sponsor recovery. It keeps support incidents, champion
coverage, executive alignment, and proof artifacts in the same review surface.

### Renewal timing checkpoint 1

The guardrail review should compare account replay evidence with renewal timing and
expansion signals. Managers can use the outcome to decide whether an account belongs
in an expansion motion, a protect motion, or a direct intervention queue. Each entry
must have a committed next step so the forecast call does not become a passive list.
